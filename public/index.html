How it works

1. ScoreA Random Forest gives each transaction a fraud probability.
2. DecideUp to 20% is approved, 80% or more is flagged, and the middle goes to a person.
3. LearnReviewer decisions are saved to the feedback log for later analysis.

Limitations

  • V1 to V28 are anonymised by the dataset, so only samples or uploaded files give meaningful scores.
  • The ₹ and IST view is a demo assumption. The model uses the original values.
  • The feedback log lives in this browser and does not retrain the model.
  • Frauds scored below 20% are approved automatically, so confident mistakes are not caught.
